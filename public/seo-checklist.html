How to use this checklist

Work through each section in order — Technical first, then On-page, and so on. Each item has a priority tag: Critical fix these before anything else, Important high ROI once the Critical items are done, Ongoing maintain these month-to-month. 01 — Technical SEO

32 checks

If Google can't crawl and index your site correctly, none of your content or links will help. Fix this layer first — it's the foundation everything else stands on.

Crawlability & Indexing
Verify your site is indexed by GoogleType site:yourdomain.com in Google. If you see your pages, you're indexed. Zero results means a serious block.↗ Fix: Check Google Search Console → Pages → "Not indexed" for reasons.
Critical
Check robots.txt is not blocking important pagesVisit yourdomain.com/robots.txt. Make sure no critical URLs (like /services/ or /blog/) are blocked with Disallow: /.
Critical
Submit an XML sitemap to Google Search ConsoleYour sitemap should list every important page. Go to Search Console → Sitemaps and submit the URL (usually /sitemap.xml or /sitemap_index.xml).
Critical
Fix all pages returning 404 errorsBroken pages waste crawl budget and hurt user experience. In Search Console → Pages, look for "Not found (404)" and either restore the pages or redirect them.
Critical
Fix all 3xx redirect chains and loopsEvery extra redirect step slows down loading and leaks authority. A redirect should go from A → B, never A → B → C.↗ Tool: Screaming Frog (free for <500 URLs) shows redirect chains.
Important
Canonicalise duplicate contentAdd a <link rel="canonical"> tag to each page pointing to the preferred version. Common duplicates: HTTP vs HTTPS, trailing slash vs no trailing slash, www vs non-www.
Critical
Ensure only one version of your domain works (www vs non-www)Pick one version and redirect the other to it permanently (301). Check by typing both versions in a browser.
Critical
Check every page has a unique, crawlable URLAvoid duplicate URLs generated by session IDs, filters or tracking parameters. Use URL parameters in Search Console to tell Google which ones to ignore.
Important
No important pages are blocked by noindex tagsSearch for noindex in your CMS or page HTML. Service pages, blog posts, and location pages should never have a noindex directive unless intentional.
Critical
Verify pagination is handled correctlyIf your blog or category pages paginate (/blog/2/, /blog/3/ etc.), each page should have a self-referencing canonical. Avoid infinite-scroll without proper URL updates.
Important
HTTPS & Security
Your entire site runs on HTTPSEvery page — not just the homepage — should start with https://. Google treats HTTPS as a light ranking signal and Chrome warns users on HTTP pages.
Critical
SSL certificate is valid and not expiring soonCheck your certificate expiry date at SSLShopper. Expired certificates destroy trust and may block crawling.
Critical
HTTP pages redirect permanently to HTTPS (301)Test: type http://yourdomain.com in a browser. You should be redirected to https:// automatically.
Critical
Page Speed & Core Web Vitals
Run PageSpeed Insights on your homepage and top 5 pagesVisit pagespeed.web.dev. Aim for a score above 70 on mobile. Note each failing audit — prioritise LCP, INP and CLS.
Tool
Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) under 2.5 secondsLCP measures how long the main content (usually your hero image or headline) takes to appear. Common fixes: preload the hero image, use a CDN, optimise server response time.
Critical
Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) below 0.1Layout shift happens when elements move after loading (e.g. images without set dimensions). Always set width and height on every image and embedded video.
Important
Interaction to Next Paint (INP) below 200msReplaces FID in 2024. Measures how quickly the page reacts to clicks and taps. Fix by reducing JavaScript execution time and splitting long tasks.
Important
All images are compressed and in a modern format (WebP or AVIF)JPEG and PNG files are typically 2–5× larger than WebP. Use Squoosh or your CMS to convert. Never upload images wider than 2000px for a blog post.
Critical
Images have explicit width and height attributesPrevents layout shift. Your CMS or framework should do this automatically — check the source of your pages to verify.
Important
Lazy-load images below the foldAdd loading="lazy" to every image not visible on page load. Do NOT add it to your hero image — that will delay LCP.
Important
Render-blocking JavaScript is deferred or eliminatedAdd defer or async to non-critical scripts. Move scripts to the bottom of the <body> or use a tag manager that loads asynchronously.
Important
CSS is minified and unused CSS is removedUse a tool like PurgeCSS to remove CSS rules that aren't used. Minify the remaining stylesheet. Many page builders load thousands of unused CSS rules.
Ongoing
Server Time to First Byte (TTFB) is under 600msMeasure in PageSpeed Insights under "Server response times". If it's slow, switch to a faster host, enable caching, or use a CDN like Cloudflare.
Important
Mobile & Accessibility
Site passes Google's Mobile-Friendly TestGoogle indexes mobile-first. Test at search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly. Fix any viewport or tap-target issues.
Critical
No horizontal scrolling on mobileView your site on a real phone or using Chrome DevTools device mode. Any element wider than the viewport breaks layout and signals poor mobile experience.
Critical
Font sizes are at least 14px on mobileText smaller than 14px fails readability tests and will be flagged in PageSpeed Insights under "Text too small to read".
Important
Tap targets (buttons, links) are at least 44×44pxToo-small tap targets are a common mobile usability issue in Search Console. Buttons and links need enough space for a finger tap.
Important
Structured Data (Schema)
Organization or LocalBusiness schema on every pageTells Google your business name, address, phone, opening hours and logo. Validate at validator.schema.org.
Important
WebSite schema with Sitelinks Search Box (optional)Adds a search box to your Google result. Requires a working site search and the WebSite schema with potentialAction.
Ongoing
BreadcrumbList schema on all interior pagesBreadcrumbs appear in Google results under the page title. They help users understand your site structure and can improve click-through rates.
Important
Article or BlogPosting schema on every blog postInclude datePublished, dateModified, author (with name and URL), headline and image. Helps Google identify fresh content.
Important
FAQPage schema on pages with FAQ sectionsCan unlock expanded FAQ results in Google — more space on the results page = more clicks. Only mark up questions that genuinely appear on the page.
Ongoing
Review schema only where genuine reviews appear on-pageNever mark up reviews from third-party platforms (Google, Trustpilot) or reviews that don't appear on the page itself — it violates Google's guidelines.
Important
All schema passes the Rich Results TestTest every schema type at search.google.com/test/rich-results. Fix any errors or warnings before deploying.
Tool

02 — On-Page SEO

28 checks

Each page should be built around one primary search term and structured to match what the searcher expects to find. This section covers every on-page element Google reads.

Keyword Targeting
Every important page targets one primary keywordAvoid keyword cannibalisation — multiple pages fighting for the same term. Use a simple spreadsheet: URL → Primary keyword → Search volume → Current rank.
Critical
Primary keyword appears in the page title (title tag)The title tag is the single most important on-page signal. The keyword should appear near the beginning if possible.
Critical
Primary keyword appears in the H1 headingThere should be exactly one H1 per page. It should be descriptive and contain your primary keyword naturally.
Critical
Primary keyword appears within the first 100 wordsGoogle gives slightly more weight to text near the top of a page. State clearly what the page is about in the opening paragraph.
Important
Related and semantic terms are used naturally throughoutModern search engines understand synonyms and related concepts. Write naturally for humans — don't repeat the exact keyword phrase artificially.
Important
Keyword appears in the URL slugKeep URLs short, descriptive and lowercase, with hyphens between words: /services/local-seo/ not /page?id=42.
Important
Title Tags & Meta Descriptions
Every page has a unique title tag (50–60 characters)Titles over 60 characters get cut off in search results. Include your brand name at the end: "Primary Keyword | Brand Name".
Critical
Every page has a unique meta description (140–160 characters)Meta descriptions don't directly affect rankings but affect click-through rate significantly. Write them as a mini advertisement for the page.
Important
No two pages share the same title or meta descriptionDuplicate metadata dilutes authority and makes it harder for Google to understand how pages differ from each other.
Important
Title tags include a clear value proposition or benefitThe best title tags answer: "Why should I click this?" Compare: "SEO Services" vs "SEO Services That Get More Calls | TechTide SEO".
Important
Content Quality
Content matches the searcher's intent (informational, commercial, navigational, transactional)Google the target keyword and study the top 3–5 results. Are they guides, product pages, comparison posts? Match that format exactly.
Critical
Content demonstrates E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust)Include the author's name, credentials or bio. Cite sources. Use real examples, data or case studies. Avoid generic, fluffy writing.
Critical
Content is more useful than the current top-ranking pagesRead your competitors' top pages. What questions do they not answer? What detail is missing? Your page should be the most complete answer available.
Important
Key pages have been refreshed in the last 12 monthsStale content loses rankings over time. Set a quarterly reminder to update statistics, examples, screenshots and any outdated advice.
Ongoing
Long content uses clear H2 and H3 headings throughoutHeadings break up content for readers and help Google understand structure. Use H2 for main sections, H3 for sub-points within those sections.
Important
Every image has descriptive alt textAlt text helps visually impaired users and tells Google what an image shows. Be descriptive and natural: "A Google Business Profile map showing a local business in Lagos" not "image1.jpg".
Important
No thin content pages (under 300 words with no unique value)Thin pages can drag down the overall quality of your site. Either expand them, merge them into a more comprehensive page, or add a noindex tag if they must exist.
Important
Internal Linking
Every page links out to at least 2–3 related pages on your own siteInternal links pass authority between pages and help Google discover content. Guide users from blog posts to service pages and from service pages to the contact or audit form.
Critical
No orphaned pages (pages with zero internal links pointing to them)Google can struggle to find pages that aren't linked from anywhere. Check in Search Console → Links → Internal links.↗ Tool: Screaming Frog — "Inlinks" column.
Important
Internal links use descriptive anchor text (not "click here")Anchor text signals what the linked page is about. "Read our local SEO guide" is far more informative to Google than "click here" or "learn more".
Important
Your most important pages receive the most internal linksInternal links act like votes between your own pages. Make sure your highest-value service pages are linked from the homepage, blog posts and other key pages.
Important
No broken internal linksBroken internal links waste crawl budget and create a poor user experience. Check using Screaming Frog or Search Console → Crawl → Links.
Critical
Calls to Action
Every page has one clear primary call to actionWhat do you want a visitor to do when they finish reading? Book a call, request a quote, download the checklist? Make it obvious, above the fold where possible.
Critical
Long-form pages have a CTA at the mid-point and at the bottomMany readers don't scroll to the end. A mid-page CTA catches those who are already convinced before they finish reading.
Important

03 — Local SEO

24 checks

Local SEO determines whether your business appears in the Google Maps pack — the three business results shown above organic results for searches with local intent. This is often the highest-value traffic source for service businesses.

Google Business Profile
Profile is claimed, verified and ownership is confirmedIf you can't find your profile, search Google Maps for your business. Unclaimed profiles can be edited by anyone.
Critical
Business name exactly matches your real-world nameDo not stuff keywords into your business name ("Lagos Plumbers - Fast 24hr Service"). This violates Google's guidelines and risks suspension.
Critical
Primary and secondary categories are set correctlyYour primary category is the most important local SEO signal. Research which category your top competitors use. Add all relevant secondary categories.
Critical
Business description is complete (750 characters) with natural keywordsWrite a genuine description of what you do, who you help and what makes you different. Include your main services and location naturally.
Important
Opening hours are accurate and updated for holidaysIncorrect hours are a top cause of bad reviews and lost customers. Update special hours for public holidays in advance.
Critical
At least 10 high-quality real photos addedPhotos of your premises, team and work increase trust dramatically. Google prioritises profiles with more photos. Add new ones monthly.
Important
All services listed with descriptions and prices where applicableThe Services section acts as a secondary keyword source. List each service with a short, accurate description.
Important
Q&A section is populated with real common questionsAdd and answer your own Q&As before customers do. Questions and answers are indexed by Google and appear in your profile.
Ongoing
Google Posts published at least twice a monthPosts keep your profile fresh and can feature offers, events or new blog articles. They appear directly in your Google result.
Ongoing
Booking or appointment link added (if applicable)Adding a booking link directly in your profile reduces friction and increases conversion. Use your scheduling tool URL.
Important
Reviews
You have a direct review link to share with customersIn your Google Business Profile dashboard, find and copy your review link. Shorten it with a QR code for print or WhatsApp messages.
Critical
You have a process for asking every happy customer for a reviewThe best time to ask is immediately after a successful job. A simple WhatsApp message with your review link works for most service businesses.
Critical
Every review — positive or negative — has a reply within 48 hoursReplying to reviews shows potential customers and Google that you're active and engaged. Never argue with a negative reviewer publicly.
Important
Your replies to reviews mention services and your location naturallyReview replies are indexed by Google. "Thank you for choosing our [service] in [city]" adds keyword signals to your profile.
Ongoing
You have more reviews than your nearest competitorsReview volume (not just rating) is a significant local ranking factor. Aim for at least 10–20 reviews before you expect to appear in the map pack.
Important
Local Citations & Consistency
NAP (name, address, phone) is identical everywhere onlineEven small differences ("St." vs "Street", "+234" vs "0234") confuse Google's local algorithm. Run a citation audit to find inconsistencies.↗ Tools: BrightLocal, Whitespark, or manual searches.
Critical
Listed on the key directories for your country and industryNigeria: VConnect, ConnectNigeria, Nairaland business listings. Worldwide: Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook Business. Industry directories relevant to your niche.
Important
LocalBusiness schema on your website matches your Google profile exactlyYour website's structured data should mirror your Google Business Profile: same name, address, phone and opening hours.
Important
Address and phone number visible on your website (not just in an image)Put your NAP in plain text in the footer and on the contact page. Text is crawlable; text inside images is not.
Critical
Location Pages
Dedicated location or area page for each area you serveIf you serve Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, each city should have its own page with unique, locally relevant content — not copy-pasted text with the city name swapped.
Important
Location pages include local landmarks, neighbourhoods or specific referencesGenuine local references (nearby streets, local events, community context) signal to Google that your page serves that area authentically.
Important

04 — Content SEO

22 checks

Content is how you earn rankings for the searches your potential customers make before they're ready to buy. A good content strategy builds compounding traffic over time.

Keyword Research
You have a master keyword list for your businessMinimum: your core service keywords, location modifiers, competitor brand terms and 10–20 question-style keywords ("how to…", "what is…", "best…").↗ Tools: Google Keyword Planner (free), Ahrefs, Semrush, Ubersuggest.
Critical
Each keyword is mapped to exactly one page on your siteUse a keyword map spreadsheet: URL | Primary keyword | Monthly searches | Current position. Update it every 3 months.
Critical
You target long-tail and question keywords, not just head termsHead terms ("SEO services") are competitive and broad. Long-tail terms ("affordable SEO for small businesses in Lagos") are easier to rank and more likely to convert.
Important
You've checked what keywords you already rank for in Search ConsoleGo to Search Console → Performance → Queries. Look for keywords where you rank positions 4–20 — these are your best quick-win opportunities.
Tool
Content Strategy
You have pillar pages for each core topic your business coversA pillar page is a comprehensive guide on a broad topic (e.g. "Complete Guide to Local SEO in Nigeria") with links to more specific supporting articles.
Important
You publish new content on a consistent, realistic scheduleConsistency matters more than volume. Two high-quality pieces a month, every month, beats ten pieces in January and nothing for six months.
Ongoing
Content covers the full customer journey (awareness → consideration → decision)Awareness: "What is SEO?" | Consideration: "Local SEO vs PPC — which is better?" | Decision: "Best SEO agency in Lagos". All three stages need content.
Important
Every piece of content has a clear goal (traffic, leads, links, brand)Before writing, define what success looks like. Content written without a clear goal tends to be unfocused and underperforms.
Important
Content Quality & Maintenance
All statistics and data in your content are current and sourcedOutdated stats (especially years-old percentages) make your content look unreliable. Link to the primary source, not a secondary site that linked to the original.
Ongoing
You have a content refresh schedule for pages ranking positions 4–20Pages in these positions have proven they can rank — they just need improvement. Updating them is usually faster and higher ROI than creating new content.
Ongoing
No keyword stuffing — keyword density stays natural (1–2%)Write for humans first. If the keyword sounds forced or awkward, rephrase using a synonym. Google's algorithms are sophisticated enough to recognise over-optimisation.
Important
Content written by or reviewed by a genuine subject matter expertGoogle's E-E-A-T guidelines reward first-hand experience. AI-generated content published without expert review, fact-checking or unique perspective risks ranking poorly on competitive topics.
Critical
Each article has an author bio linking to their credentials or social profileAuthor information is a key trust signal. A named, credentialed author (with a LinkedIn link) is significantly more trustworthy than "the team".
Important
Optimising for AI Search (AEO)
Content directly and concisely answers common questionsAI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) summarise information from pages that answer questions clearly. Structure answers in the first paragraph, then expand with detail.
Important
Your brand and services are mentioned on reputable third-party sitesAI assistants draw from sites they trust. Being featured in industry directories, local news, award sites and partner pages increases your chance of being named in AI answers.
Important
FAQs on service pages use the exact phrasing customers type into AI toolsAsk ChatGPT or Gemini what questions people ask about your service category. Write FAQs that answer those exact questions word-for-word.
Ongoing

05 — Link Building & Authority

18 checks

Links from trustworthy, relevant sites tell Google your site deserves to rank. Quality always beats quantity — one link from a respected local newspaper is worth more than 50 links from unrelated directories.

Link Profile Audit
You know how many sites link to you and who they areCheck in Search Console → Links → Top linking sites. For more detail, use Ahrefs (free tier), Semrush or Moz Link Explorer.↗ Tool: Google Search Console → Links (free).
Tool
No manual action or penalty is applied to your siteCheck Search Console → Security & Manual Actions. A manual action for unnatural links means you have toxic backlinks that need to be removed or disavowed.
Critical
Toxic or spammy links have been disavowedIf you've received links from link farms, adult sites or irrelevant foreign directories, use Google's Disavow tool (Search Console) to tell Google to ignore them.
Critical
Your link profile has diversity in anchor textHealthy profiles have a mix of branded anchors ("TechTide SEO"), generic ("click here", "website"), and keyword-rich anchors. Over-optimised anchors (100% keyword anchors) are a red flag.
Important
Link Acquisition
You have identified your top 10 competitors' best linksSites that link to your competitors but not to you are your best link opportunities. Filter by relevance and authority, then pursue each one systematically.↗ Tool: Ahrefs Link Intersect or Semrush Backlink Gap.
Tool
Your business is listed on industry-specific directories and associationsTrade associations, chamber of commerce pages, professional bodies and industry platforms often give followed links to members. These are some of the safest and most relevant links available.
Important
Partners, suppliers and clients have linked to your websiteAsk people you already have a relationship with to link to you. A supplier's "trusted partners" page or a client's "resources" page are natural and easy wins.
Important
You have at least one piece of linkable content (data, tools, guides)Content that earns links naturally includes original research, free tools, comprehensive guides, and unique data. Generic blog posts rarely earn links.
Important
You monitor brand mentions that don't link back to youSet up Google Alerts for your brand name. When someone mentions you without a link, reach out politely and ask them to add one.
Ongoing
You never buy links or participate in link exchange schemesPaid links and reciprocal link schemes violate Google's guidelines. Penalties — manual or algorithmic — can take months to recover from and can be catastrophic for rankings.
Critical
Digital PR
You have pitched expert commentary to at least one journalist or publicationJournalists constantly need expert sources. A brief, helpful response to a relevant query (HARO, ResponseSource, or direct outreach) can earn high-authority editorial links.
Ongoing
Your company milestones, data or insights have been pitched as news storiesBusiness wins, survey data, staff hires, and community involvement can all be newsworthy at the local or industry level. Build a relationship with local journalists.
Ongoing
Guest articles have been published on relevant industry sitesGenuine guest articles on sites your customers read are valuable — both for links and for reaching new audiences. Avoid low-quality guest post farms.
Ongoing

06 — Measurement & Monthly Reporting

16 checks

If you can't measure it, you can't improve it. This section covers the tools to set up once and the reports to review every month so you know whether your SEO is working.

Tools Setup (one-time)
Google Search Console is installed and verifiedFree from Google. Provides keyword rankings, click-through rates, crawl errors, index coverage and link data. Install before anything else.
Critical
Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is installed and tracking correctlySet up conversion events for phone clicks, form submissions, and any "thank you" pages. Without conversions tracked, you can't connect SEO to business results.
Critical
Google Business Profile Insights are being reviewed monthlyYour Profile dashboard shows how many people called, clicked for directions, visited your website or viewed your photos from Google. Track trends month-to-month.
Important
Rank tracking is set up for your top 20 keywordsTrack your priority keywords weekly. Search Console is good for broad trends; a dedicated rank tracker (SerpRobot, AccuRanker, or Semrush) is better for daily precision.↗ Tools: SerpRobot (low cost), Google Search Console (free).
Tool
Backlink monitoring is activeYou need to know when you gain or lose important links. Set up alerts in Ahrefs, Semrush or Google Search Console (Links report, checked monthly).
Important
Monthly Review Checklist
Review organic clicks and impressions vs previous monthIn Search Console → Performance → Search results. Are clicks trending up? If impressions rise but clicks don't, your titles and meta descriptions need work.
Ongoing
Identify which pages gained or lost the most rankingsSort your rank tracker by position change. Investigate pages that dropped significantly — was there a content change, a technical issue, or a Google algorithm update?
Ongoing
Check Search Console for new crawl errors or indexing issuesGo to Pages → All pages → filter by "Not indexed". New issues can appear after site updates. Address them within the same month.
Ongoing
Track conversions: calls, form submissions, map direction requestsRankings are vanity — conversions are reality. Report on actual business results: how many leads came from organic search this month vs last month?
Ongoing
Check Core Web Vitals report for any new failing pagesIn Search Console → Experience → Core Web Vitals. Site updates, new embeds and third-party scripts can cause previously passing pages to fail.
Ongoing
Review Google Business Profile review count and ratingAre you gaining reviews? Is your average rating stable? An unexpected drop in reviews or rating needs immediate attention — possible fake review attack or customer service issue.
Ongoing
Identify quick-win keywords ranking positions 4–20These pages have proven intent-match and are close to the top. Pick the two or three with the highest potential and refresh or expand them that month.
Ongoing
Check for and respond to any manual actions in Search ConsoleSearch Console → Security & Manual Actions. Manual actions are rare but serious — they require a reconsideration request after the issue is fixed.
Ongoing
Pro tip — build a one-page monthly SEO report: Pull three numbers from each tool — clicks (Search Console), leads (GA4) and map views (Business Profile). Compare to the previous month. Note what you worked on and what you plan to do next month. This is more useful than a 40-page PDF and takes 20 minutes to produce.
