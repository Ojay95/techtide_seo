## Start with intent

Before editing a page, decide what one search it should answer and what the searcher wants. Search the term yourself and look at what already ranks. If the top results are guides, a service page won't compete. Give each page one main topic, and never target the same search with two pages.

## Page elements

- **Title tag:** the main keyword near the front, written to earn the click, and about 60 characters or fewer.
- **Meta description:** about 150 characters with a clear benefit and next step. It doesn't affect rankings directly, but it affects clicks.
- **One H1:** it matches the page's topic and the title's intent.
- **Subheadings:** H2 and H3 headings organise the page and cover related questions.
- **URL:** short, readable and descriptive, with no dates or clutter.
- **Image alt text:** it describes the image for people using screen readers.

## Content quality

- It answers the question completely and gets to the point early.
- It shows real experience: photos, examples, prices, process and results.
- It includes the questions people ask, such as those in "People also ask" in Google results.
- It is accurate, up to date and easy to read.
- It uses the main keyword and related terms naturally, without stuffing.

## Internal links

- Link to the page from related pages, using descriptive link text.
- Link from the page to relevant guides and services.
- No page is orphaned, meaning nothing links to it.

## Conversion

- The main call to action is visible without scrolling, and it is clear what happens next.
- Phone numbers are clickable on mobile.
- Forms ask only for what you need.
- Proof such as reviews, case studies or credentials sits near the call to action.

## Final check

- The page loads quickly on a phone.
- Title, description and headings are unique across the site.
- The page is indexed, and you've noted its starting position so you can measure progress.
