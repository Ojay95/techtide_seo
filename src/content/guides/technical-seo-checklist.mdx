## How to use this checklist

Work through the sections in order. Crawling and indexing come first, because nothing else matters if Google can't reach your pages. Re-check monthly, since sites change.

## Crawling and indexing

- Your most important pages are indexed. Check in Google Search Console under Indexing, then Pages.
- Your robots.txt file does not block key pages, styles or scripts.
- You have an XML sitemap listing only live, indexable pages, and it is submitted in Search Console.
- No important page has an accidental noindex tag, which often survives from a staging site.
- Every page has a canonical tag pointing to its preferred address.

## Redirects and errors

- Broken pages (404 errors) with links or traffic redirect to the closest relevant page.
- Redirects are single steps, not chains.
- The site uses one version of the address: https, and either www or non-www.
- There are no server errors (5xx) in your crawl.

## Speed and Core Web Vitals

- Largest Contentful Paint is 2.5 seconds or better.
- Interaction to Next Paint is 200 milliseconds or better.
- Cumulative Layout Shift is 0.1 or lower.
- Images are compressed, in a modern format such as WebP, and have set dimensions.
- Unused plugins and scripts are removed, and caching is on.

Test with PageSpeed Insights and check the field data in Search Console.

## Mobile and security

- The site works well on a phone, with readable text and spaced tap targets.
- The whole site loads over HTTPS with no mixed content warnings.
- Intrusive popups don't block the content.

## Structure and links

- Important pages are within three clicks of the home page.
- URLs are short, readable and lowercase.
- Every page can be reached through internal links, with no orphan pages.
- Breadcrumbs show where a page sits in the site.

## Structured data

- Your business has Organization or LocalBusiness markup with accurate details.
- Pages use fitting markup, such as Service, Article or Breadcrumb.
- Markup validates in Google's Rich Results Test and matches what visitors see.

## Monitoring

- Search Console is set up and checked weekly for errors.
- You re-crawl the site monthly with a tool such as Screaming Frog.
