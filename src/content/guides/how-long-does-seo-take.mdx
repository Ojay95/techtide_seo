## The short answer

Local improvements can appear within weeks, while competitive searches often take four to six months or longer. No one can promise an exact date, because search engines decide rankings and many factors are outside anyone's control.

## A realistic timeline

| Period | What usually happens |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1 to 4 | Audit, baseline measurement and the first technical and profile fixes. Little change in rankings yet. |
| Months 2 to 3 | Improved pages and local profiles start to be reindexed. Early movement for lower-competition and local searches. |
| Months 4 to 6 | Content and links build up. Steadier growth in visibility, clicks and enquiries. |
| Months 6 to 12 | Compounding results as pages age and authority grows. Gains for more competitive searches. |

These are typical patterns, not promises. Your own timeline depends on the factors below.

## What affects the timeline

- **Competition:** crowded markets take longer.
- **Starting point:** a site with serious technical problems needs repair first.
- **Site age and authority:** established sites often move faster than new ones.
- **Content and links:** the more useful pages and trusted links you earn, the faster you progress.
- **Local or national:** local searches are usually quicker to win than national ones.
- **Consistency:** SEO rewards steady work over bursts.

## What can speed things up

Fixing indexing and speed problems early, making sure pages match what people search, completing your Google Business Profile, and collecting reviews all produce faster early gains.

## Be wary of

- Anyone guaranteeing a ranking or a date.
- Shortcuts such as bought links, which can lead to penalties and set you back.
- Judging results too early. Give the work a fair few months before deciding.

## Measure what matters

Track calls, leads and sales alongside rankings. Rankings can move before revenue does, and revenue is what the work is for.
